# v166 combat feedback and respawn

## Changes

- Guest observation HP now changes only on eight friendly hit markers, with
  the final hero hit at 26.6s. No continuous drain while charging, waiting or
  during the guardian's attacks. Real HP snapshots remain authoritative;
  confirmed decreases are released visually at a visible friendly impact.
- A confirmed final hit has a 0.6s visible release, followed by a 3.2s defeat:
  recoil, weighted kneel/collapse, stone chips, grounded dust and fading embers.
  A paused death survives the host's cooldown transition; a new round or
  account-domain change clears it. No local callback awards a reward.
- Chinese skill names, including a full-width exclamation mark, appear in a
  caster-local bubble. The previous screen-centred ultimate strip is removed.
  The bottom long HP line has no percentage or heavy enclosing frame.
- Damage numbers use the same displayed-HP change, impact and pauseable clock.
  Real numbers are the already-confirmed decrease, not randomly invented hits.
  Observation alone uses a 1000-point display scale. Hero ultimate numbers are
  emphasized; every number expires after 1.2 animation seconds.
- Five surveyed encounter centres: (5,-12), (2,-15), (8,-15), (2,-9), (8,-9).
  Initial placement retains the accepted baseline. Later rounds choose a
  different safe site once per round, moving the trio's stations together.
  Repeated snapshots and HP updates do not reroll placement. Every candidate
  is rechecked with the live ground/obstacle callbacks for the full catalogue.
  No eligible alternative fails closed instead of spawning into an obstacle.

The approved world, water, wildlife, all 13 source actor shards and all three
quality tiers are unchanged. No backend, account, timer, reward rule, production
service or production deployment is changed by this presentation update.
