Use case: compositing. Transparent web illustration asset with a real alpha channel: a horizontal rip torn through a web page, in the torn-paper comic style of the reference image.

Composition: one wide gash, about 3 times as wide as it is tall, tilted only about 3 degrees (right end slightly higher), spanning nearly the full canvas width and centred vertically. The lime opening is wide: at least 45% of the rip's height through the middle 70% of its length, narrowing only near the two sharp torn points at the ends.

Inside the rip: acid lime green (#c5ff32) with fine halftone dots, slightly deeper lime (#93cc1c) near the lips. Leave the inside EMPTY — it will get lettering later: no text, no letters, no symbols, no characters inside.

Both lips of the rip: the torn edges of the cream-white page — thick, fibrous, ragged paper edges curled outward with visible paper thickness, pale blue-grey shading inside the curls and soft shadows falling into the rip, the same feeling as the torn edge in the reference. A few small white paper scraps fly off the left end, and 3–4 short navy ink speed lines trail on the far left.

Everything outside the rip and its torn paper lips is fully transparent. No page content, no UI, no frame, no border, no watermark, no text anywhere.

The transparent area must be a real alpha channel (fully transparent pixels): never paint a checkerboard, grey or white background there.
